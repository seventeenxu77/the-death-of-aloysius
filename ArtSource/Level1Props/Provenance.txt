BACKGROUND REMOVAL ONLY for a Unity 2D sprite sheet. Precisely preserve the THREE existing approved props and their shapes, dimensions, proportions, placement and spacing: small low bed WITHOUT headboard at lower left, simple closed pointed-arch window WITHOUT hinge decorations at center, tall closed pointed-arch door with ONE tiny straight lever handle at right. Remove the entire ochre parchment background to TRUE TRANSPARENCY, including the empty space between and underneath objects. The thin ochre separation lines within the bed, the window frame/central seam, door frame and tiny handle should become transparent cutouts so the game background shows through them. Keep the actual silhouettes solid dark near-black with crisp antialiased edges. Do NOT redesign, resize, move, add details, remove the door handle, add a headboard, change object count, add shadows, add lighting, or add a background. Preserve this exact full wide canvas and clear gaps between the three separate assets for sprite slicing. No text, no checkerboard baked into image, no labels, no border. Only three clean black silhouettes on genuine transparent alpha.
